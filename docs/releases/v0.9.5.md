# CodeY CMS 0.9.5

CodeY CMS 0.9.5 keeps newly generated and self-hosted websites free from
published demo catalog and article content.

## Highlights

- Creates runtime roles, module settings, navigation, and an editable Home page
  during normal bootstrap without publishing a sample post or product.
- Leaves sample coupons, shipping rates, and tax rules out of customer shops.
- Keeps representative demo content available for development and test fixtures
  through the explicit `CODEY_SEED_DEMO_CONTENT=true` opt-in.
- Preserves existing customer content and configuration during upgrades.

## Contracts

- `websiteSpec: "1.0"`
- `builder: "1.0"`
- `exportedSiteAcceptance: "1.0"`
- `operationalDiagnostics: "1.0"`
- `offsiteBackupReadiness: "1.0"`
- `supplyChain: "1.0"`

## Upgrade Notes

No database migration is required. Existing starter records are not removed
automatically because they may have been edited into real customer content.
Fresh installs and future idempotent seed runs leave demo content disabled by
default.
