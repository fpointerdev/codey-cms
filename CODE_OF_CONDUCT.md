# CodeY Community Conduct

CodeY welcomes practical, respectful collaboration from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and product behavior directly without attacking people.
- Give enough context for others to reproduce a problem or evaluate a proposal.
- Respect privacy, security disclosures, and confidential customer information.
- Accept maintainers' responsibility for compatibility, safety, and project scope.
- Help keep documentation and workflows understandable to nontechnical users.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

For community conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. For vulnerabilities, use the private process in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect contributors and users.

This policy applies to project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review spaces, and project events.
